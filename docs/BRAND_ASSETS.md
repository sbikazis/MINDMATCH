# MINDMATCH brand assets

## App mark

The MINDMATCH mark combines two people into an M-shaped connection, with a heart at the center to represent friendship and compatibility.

- Deep background: `#0B091B`
- Pink: `#E83FA5`
- Blue: `#3B9EF2`
- Violet: `#8E55E8`

## Platform status

- Android: legacy vector launcher icon and Android 8+ adaptive launcher icon are included.
- iOS: editable SVG brand artwork is included in `MindMatchBrand.imageset`.
- Still required: export and configure the required 1024 × 1024 PNG in the iOS `AppIcon.appiconset`, then verify the icon in Xcode and on physical devices.

The editable SVG is artwork, not a substitute for the App Store PNG icon.
