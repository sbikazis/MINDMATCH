# MINDMATCH Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 24 November 2026**

MINDMATCH is an offline-first question game. The app remains offline-first and does not require an account or use a MINDMATCH-owned application backend. It now integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy choices where required.

## Information the app handles

- Your quiz answers are processed on your device to calculate a match.
- A challenge code contains the creator's selected answer indexes and question identifiers. It is encoded for sharing, not encrypted. Anyone who receives the code can read the encoded choices.
- When you choose to share a challenge or result, the app passes the content to the operating system's share sheet. The destination service you select handles it under its own privacy policy.
- When you share a result card as an image, the app temporarily creates the image in the device's app cache so it can be attached to the share sheet. It is not uploaded by MINDMATCH.

## Advertising and third-party data

MINDMATCH displays Google Mobile Ads banner and native ads, and may display occasional interstitial ads between completed quizzes. Rewarded ads are supported by the ad service but must only be shown when a real, optional in-app reward is offered.

When ad requests are made, Google Mobile Ads may collect and share the device IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), user product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers including the Android advertising ID. These data may be used for advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention. Data collection can vary with device settings, consent choices, and enabled ad features. Google documents that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. See [Google Play data disclosure for Mobile Ads](https://developers.google.com/ad-manager/mobile-ads-sdk/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests updated consent information at app launch and displays a consent form when required. Where required, an in-app privacy-options entry point lets users revisit their choices. Whether ads are personalized depends on applicable requirements and the user's choices.

Quiz answers and challenge codes are not sent to a MINDMATCH server for advertising. When you share a challenge or result, the operating system share sheet passes the content to the destination you choose.

## Children

MINDMATCH is a casual social game. The publisher must set the appropriate age/content rating in each app store before release. Do not share private or sensitive information in challenge codes.

## Changes

This notice describes the current release candidate. The publisher must keep this notice and the Google Play Data safety form aligned with the exact SDK version, enabled ad features, and AdMob/UMP configuration before every release.
