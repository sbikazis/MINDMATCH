Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how the MINDMATCH mobile application (“MINDMATCH”, “we”, or “the app”) handles information. It describes the current app design and should be updated whenever the app's features or data practices change.

In short: MINDMATCH is designed to work offline and does not require you to create an account. Your quiz answers are kept on your device unless you choose to share a challenge code.

1. Information you enter

You may provide answers to quiz questions. The app uses those answers on your device to calculate a match result. The current first-release design does not require a name, email address, phone number, or account registration.

2. Challenge codes and sharing

When you create and share a challenge, the challenge code contains the creator's selected answers and question identifiers so another person can answer the same questions and compare results. Anyone who receives that code may be able to read the information encoded in it. Share it only with people you trust. Challenge codes are encoded for portability; they are not encrypted or a secure way to transmit sensitive information.

Sharing is initiated by you through your device's share interface. The app does not control how third-party messaging or social apps store, process, or further share content you send through them. Their own privacy policies apply.

3. Collection, analytics, and advertising

MINDMATCH uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner, native, and occasional interstitial advertisements. Rewarded ads are reserved for optional features that explicitly offer a reward. The app does not send quiz answers or challenge codes to its own server for advertising.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as the device IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), advertising or other device identifiers, ad views and taps or other app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The exact data processed can depend on device settings, consent choices, and the ad features used. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google's Mobile Ads privacy and data disclosure information.

Where required, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to present consent and privacy choices before ads are requested. You can change your choices using the in-app ad privacy options when that option is required. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable rules and your choices.

For more information about how Google uses data, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Storage and retention

Quiz activity is processed locally by the app. Information retained by the app remains on your device. You can clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data, subject to your device's backup settings. Challenge codes you have already shared may remain in recipients' messages or other third-party services; deleting the app does not delete those copies.

5. Permissions and device information

The app may use your operating system's share interface when you choose to share a challenge or result. The app does not need access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera for the current quiz flow.

6. Children

MINDMATCH is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not include sensitive personal information in quiz answers or challenge codes.

7. Security

Keeping quiz processing on-device reduces the information sent to us. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Challenge codes are readable data, not encrypted messages; do not use them to share sensitive information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when an updated policy is published. Please review this page periodically.

9. Contact and support

For questions about this policy or the app, use the MINDMATCH support page. Please do not post private information, passwords, or challenge codes in public support requests.
